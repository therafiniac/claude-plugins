## 6. Media & Motion

- [ ] Prerecorded video has captions and a transcript or audio description where visuals carry meaning. Audio-only content has a transcript. (1.2.x)
- [ ] Audio and video never autoplay with sound. Media controls are keyboard operable and labelled.
- [ ] Animation and parallax respect `prefers-reduced-motion`, and no animation is essential to understanding content.
- [ ] Carousels, tickers, and background video can be paused. (2.2.2)
- [ ] Any motion-based input (shake, tilt) has a normal control alternative. (2.5.4)
